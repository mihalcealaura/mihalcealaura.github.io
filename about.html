about me

I am Laura, a master's student in Digital and Interaction Design at Politecnico di Milano with a background in Industrial Design.

My work explores human-centered and systemic approaches to design, translating complex technologies into intuitive and meaningful digital experiences. Through research and interaction design, I aim to create systems that are accessible, inclusive and shaped around long-term impact.
